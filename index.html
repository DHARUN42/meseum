About the fridge museum

Fridge Museum is a fictional website that treats an ordinary household refrigerator like a museum.

Instead of selling food or sharing recipes, it documents the strange, forgotten, unidentified, and interesting things hiding inside refrigerators. Because every fridge has a story, Some are just better left unopened.
